- **border on/upon**: 濒临/接近。
  - √ *"The situation **borders on** chaos."* — 局势近乎混乱。
  - × *"The situation **borders** chaos."* — 错误，border表示"濒临"时需加on/upon。

### ❓ Quiz
- **Fill in the blank**: "They crossed the _____ into the neighboring country."
  - Answer: **border**
