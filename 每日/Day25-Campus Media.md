## Scenario Dialogue
- **Sophia:** Hey Jason, is our video idea valid for the campus contest? The rules seem strict this year.
- **Jason:** I value your concern, but yes, the theme is vital to our club's image. We just need vivid visuals to stand out.
- **Sophia:** Perfect! I found an old wagon we can use as a prop. Its wheels still work, and we can widen the scene with creative angles.
- **Jason:** Great idea! We could weave a story around it. But wait—my laptop has a virus, I can't edit anything!
- **Sophia:** Worst case scenario, we use the library computers. The width of the editing room is small, but it'll work.
- **Jason:** I wakened early today to prepare, so let's make this worth the effort. My waist hurts from carrying all the gear though.
- **Sophia:** Here, drink some water and rest. Did you clean that wound on your hand? It looks red.
- **Jason:** It's fine, just a small wound from the broken wheel. Oh look—the campus wheat field would make a perfect backdrop!
- **Sophia:** Amazing! Like a whale swimming in a golden sea. This video will be unforgettable!
