## Grammar Point

### Prepositions of Position: among vs. amongst
| Word | Usage | Example |
|------|-------|---------|
| **among** | 在…之中 | She stood **among** the crowd. (她站在人群中。) |
| **amongst** | 在…之中(=among，更正式) | **Amongst** the students, he was the tallest. (在学生中他是最高的。) |

### Word Family: anger (n) → angry (adj) → angrily (adv)
| Word | POS | Meaning | Example |
|------|-----|---------|---------|
| **anger** | n | 愤怒 | She spoke with **anger**. (她愤怒地说。) |
| **angry** | adj | 生气的 | He was **angry** about the delay. (他对延误感到生气。) |

### Verb → Noun: analyse → analysis
| Pattern | Example |
|---------|---------|
| **analyse** (v) → **analysis** (n) | We **analyse** data → Our **analysis** shows good results. (我们分析数据→我们的分析显示好结果。) |

## Quiz (Fill in the Blank)

1. "She sat _____ her friends at the booth."
   A. among   B. between   C. across   D. along
   → 答案：A (among) — 她坐在摊位的朋友们中间。

2. "A large _____ of students came to the event."
   A. number   B. amount   C. total   D. sum
   → 答案：B (amount) — 大量学生来参加活动。

3. "The artifact was from _____ times."
   A. ancient   B. modern   C. current   D. recent
   → 答案：A (ancient) — 这件文物来自古代。

4. "Can you _____ the microphone volume?"
   A. analyse   B. amplify   C. amuse   D. anchor
   → 答案：B (amplify) — 你能放大麦克风音量吗？

5. "Let me do a quick _____ of the data."
   A. analyse   B. analyst   C. analysis   D. analytic
   → 答案：C (analysis) — 让我快速分析一下数据。
