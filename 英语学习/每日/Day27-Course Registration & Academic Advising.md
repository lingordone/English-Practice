## 1. absorb

<font color=purple>┃</font> 吸收（液体、知识等） v.

/əbˈzɔːrb/
![[audio/absorb.mp3]]

### 💬 Example Sentences
1. *"Students need to absorb a lot of new knowledge during orientation week."* — 学生需要在迎新周期间吸收大量新知识。
   ![[audio/absorb_ex1.mp3]]
2. *"It takes time to absorb all the information from the professor's lecture."* — 吸收讲座中的所有知识需要时间。
   ![[audio/absorb_ex2.mp3]]

### 🗣️ Dialogue
- **Sophia: How can I absorb so much new information in just one semester?**
- **Jason: Take it step by step, and review your notes every day.**

### 📖 Grammar & Usage Note
- **absorb** 是及物动词，直接接宾语，表示吸收液体、气体或抽象知识。
  - √ *"Plants absorb water and nutrients from the soil."* — 植物从土壤中吸收水分和养分。
  - × *"The student was absorbed by the textbook."* — 表示'专心于'应用 absorbed in，不能用 by。

### ❓ Quiz
- **Fill in the blank**: "The sponge can ___ a lot of water."
  - Answer: **absorb**
