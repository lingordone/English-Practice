### 💬 Example Sentences
1. *"That's **utter nonsense**!"* — 那是一派胡言！。
   ![[audio/utter_ex1.mp3]]
2. *"The room was in **utter darkness**."* — 房间一片漆黑。。
   ![[audio/utter_ex2.mp3]]

### 🗣️ Dialogue
- **Sophia:** I used to feel **utter** despair.
- **Jason:** Now there's hope instead of **utter** darkness.

### 📖 Grammar & Usage Note
- **utter + 名词**: 完全的。修饰名词，常含贬义。
  - √ *"Utter nonsense."* — 正确。（一派胡言。）
  - × *"An utter to fail."* — 错误。（utter 是形容词，不能接不定式。）

### ❓ Quiz
- **Fill in the blank**: "That's ___ nonsense!"
  A. utter
  B. total
  C. complete
  D. absolute
  → Answer: **utter** (完全的)
