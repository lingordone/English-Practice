## 1. tempt

<font color=purple>┃</font> 引诱，诱惑；吸引 v.

/tempt/
![[audio/tempt.mp3]]

### Example Sentences
1. *"The delicious smell of fresh bread will **tempt** you to buy some."* — 新鲜面包的香味会诱惑你买一些。
   ![[audio/tempt_ex1.mp3]]
2. *"Don't let social media **tempt** you into wasting time online."* — 不要让社交媒体诱惑你在网上浪费时间。
   ![[audio/tempt_ex2.mp3]]

### Dialogue
- **Sophia: The new dessert shop really knows how to **tempt** students with their window displays.**
- **Jason: I know! I walked past three times before I finally gave in and bought a cake.**

### Grammar & Usage Note
- **tempt + sb + into + doing**: 引诱某人做某事。
  - √ *"The advertisement tempted me into buying the product."* — 广告引诱我买了这个产品。
  - × *"He tempted to steal the money."* — 他引诱偷钱。（缺少宾语）

### Quiz
- **Fill in the blank**: "The smell of coffee will ____ you into buying a cup."
  - Answer: **tempt**

### Dialogue
- **Sophia: Someone left **trash** all over the picnic area after the event.**
- **Jason: That's so disrespectful — we should organize a cleanup.**

### Grammar & Usage Note
- **trash** 作动词时意为'破坏'或'丢弃'，常用于口语。
  - √ *"Someone trashed the park after the concert."* — 有人在音乐会后把公园弄得一团糟。
  - × *"He trashed his old computer."* — 他丢弃了他的旧电脑。（正确用法）
