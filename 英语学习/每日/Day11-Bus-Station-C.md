## 语境对话

**场景描述**: 在公交总站，各种各样的人在等车——背包客问路、家长带孩子、调度员协调、警察抓骗子。

> **Ming**: Excuse me, have you seen a **lost child** near the **terminal**? I'm a **receptionist** here and a **parent** is asking for help.
> **Hao**: I saw a **toddler** crying near the **kiosk**. A **bystander** was with him. Is the **parent** a **teenager** or an adult?
> **Ming**: An adult. The **lost_child** got separated when the **parent** was buying snacks from a **vendor**.
> **Hao**: Oh no. I also saw a **scammer** selling fake tickets near the **entrance**. A **fare_inspector** and a **pedestrian** stopped him.
> **Ming**: Good. The **dispatcher** announced that a **backpacker** and a **hiker** are arriving on the next bus. There's also a **wheelchair_user** waiting at the **shelter**.
> **Hao**: I see a **companion** helping a **straggler** with heavy luggage near the **bay**.
> **Ming**: Perfect. And there's a **sightseer** with a camera taking photos of the **concourse**.
> **Hao**: Yes. Oh look — a **pickpocket** tried to steal from a **beggar**! The **informant** at the booth is calling security.
> **Ming**: Let's hurry and reunite that **lost_child** with his **parent**!

## 语法点

### 复合名词作定语（Noun Adjunct）

- 英语中常直接用名词修饰另一个名词，**不加 's**。
  - **bus terminal**（公交终点站）→ bus 直接修饰 terminal
  - **fare inspector**（查票员）→ fare 修饰 inspector
  - **ticket booth**（售票亭）→ ticket 修饰 booth

- **√** 正确：The **bus station** has many **food vendors**.
  （公交站台有很多食品摊贩。）
- **×** 错误：The **bus's station** has many **food's vendors**.
  （误用所有格，不符合复合名词习惯）

- **注意**：当复合名词中第一个名词是"人"时，通常仍用名词修饰：
  - **ticket clerk**（售票员），不说 ticket's clerk
  - **station master**（站长），不说 station's master

## 填空练习

1. "The ___ checked tickets on the bus."
   A. fare_inspector   B. fare_checker   C. ticket_master   D. coin_collector

   → 答案：A（fare_inspector = 查票员）

2. "A ___ with a heavy backpack waited at the shelter."
   A. scammer   B. hiker   C. dispatcher   D. receptionist

   → 答案：B（hiker = 徒步旅行者）

3. "The ___ coordinated bus departures from the control room."
   A. dispatcher   B. bystander   C. pedestrian   D. companion

   → 答案：A（dispatcher = 调度员）

4. "A ___ tried to sell fake tickets at the station."
   A. backpacker   B. scammer   C. informant   D. toddler

   → 答案：B（scammer = 骗子）

5. "The ___ offered to help carry the luggage."
   A. straggler   B. bystander   C. beggar   D. pickpocket

   → 答案：B（bystander = 旁观者）
