## 语境对话

**场景**: 两个朋友在火车站。Jason刚到车站，Sophia引导他找到正确的站台。

> **Sophia:** Hey Jason, I'm at the station now. Where are you?
> **Jason:** I'm on platform 5. Can you see the departure board?
> **Sophia:** Yes, it says the train leaves from track 3 in ten minutes.
> **Jason:** Oh no, I need to buy a ticket first! Where's the ticket office?
> **Sophia:** It's next to the entrance. There's also a ticket machine near the booking hall.
> **Jason:** Great, I'll use the machine. Where should I wait?
> **Sophia:** The waiting room is upstairs, or you can sit in the waiting area near your platform.
> **Jason:** I have too much luggage. Is there a luggage room?
> **Sophia:** Yes, it's opposite the information desk. Ask the staff there.
> **Jason:** Thanks! How do I get to the arrival board? My friend's train is coming in.
> **Sophia:** It's on the wall above the exit. You can also check the signal box schedule online.
> **Jason:** I'll cross the footbridge to get to my platform. The underpass is too crowded.
> **Sophia:** Good idea. After you arrive, where are you going?
> **Jason:** To the car park first, then the taxi rank to get a cab. Or maybe the bus stop is closer.
> **Sophia:** Sounds like a plan. See you soon!

## 语法点：方位介词 (Prepositions of Place)

方位介词用来表示人或物在空间中的位置关系。在火车站场景中，以下介词最为常用：

### 1. in — 在…里面
- 用于封闭空间：in the waiting room, in the booking hall
- **in + 地点**: I'm in the waiting room on the second floor.
- **in + 区域**: The car park is in the east of the station.

### 2. on — 在…上面 / 在（某层）
- 用于表面或楼层：on platform 5, on the footbridge, on the second floor
- **on + 站台/轨道**: Your train is on platform 3.
- **on + 楼层**: The ticket office is on the ground floor.

### 3. at — 在（某处）
- 用于具体地点：at the entrance, at the bus stop, at the information desk
- **at + 具体点**: Meet me at the main entrance.
- **at + 设施**: She works at the information desk.

### 4. next to / beside — 在…旁边
- The ticket machine is next to the booking hall.

### 5. opposite — 在…对面
- The luggage room is opposite the information desk.

### 6. between — 在…之间
- The signal box is between platform 1 and platform 3.

### 7. above / below — 在…上方/下方
- The departure board is above the exit.

### 8. near / close to — 在…附近
- There's a coffee shop near the waiting area.

### √ 正确用法
- The waiting room is **on** the second floor. (楼层用 on)
- Let's meet **at** the main entrance. (具体地点用 at)
- The ticket office is **next to** the booking hall. (旁边用 next to)

### × 常见错误
- ~~The waiting room is in the second floor.~~ → 楼层用 on，不是 in
- ~~Let's meet in the main entrance.~~ → 具体地点用 at，不是 in

## 填空练习

1. The train is leaving ___ platform 3 in five minutes.
   A. in   B. on   C. at   D. by
   **→ 答案：B** (platform 前用 on)

2. You can buy tickets ___ the ticket machine or ___ the ticket office.
   A. on / at   B. at / on   C. in / at   D. on / in
   **→ 答案：A** (machine 前用 on，office 前用 at)

3. The waiting room is ___ the second floor, ___ the information desk.
   A. in / next to   B. on / next to   C. at / on   D. on / in
   **→ 答案：B** (楼层用 on，旁边用 next to)

4. The luggage room is ___ the booking hall, ___ the entrance.
   A. opposite / near   B. next to / opposite   C. on / at   D. at / on
   **→ 答案：A** (对面用 opposite，附近用 near)

5. The departure board is ___ the exit, so you can see it when you leave.
   A. below   B. on   C. above   D. at
   **→ 答案：C** (上方用 above)
