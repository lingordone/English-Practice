## 语法点

### 物品名词与介词搭配（of/on/at/for + thing）

- 英语中物品名词需要搭配特定的介词，构成固定短语。
  - **the boarding pass to the flight**: 航班的登机牌
  - **the luggage tag on the suitcase**: 行李箱上的行李标签
  - **the visa for China**: 中国签证
  - **the seatbelt on the plane**: 飞机上的安全带

- **√** 正确：The boarding pass to the flight has the seat number.
  （航班的登机牌上有座位号。）
- **×** 错误：The boarding pass of the flight has the seat number.
  （表示所属关系应使用 to，而不是 of。）

- **常见搭配**：
  - **the overhead bin in the plane**: 飞机上的头顶行李舱
  - **the carry-on under the seat**: 座位下的随身行李
  - **the beverage from the cart**: 推车上的饮料
  - **the duty-free shop near the gate**: 登机口附近的免税店
  - **the headset for the flight**: 用于飞行的耳机

## 填空练习

1. "I showed my ___ at the gate."
   A. boarding pass   B. luggage tag   C. visa   D. seatbelt

   → 答案：A（boarding pass）

2. "The ___ fell off my suitcase."
   A. luggage tag   B. overhead bin   C. carry-on   D. beverage

   → 答案：A（luggage tag）

3. "Please fasten your ___."
   A. seatbelt   B. headset   C. adapter   D. eye mask

   → 答案：A（seatbelt）

4. "Put your bag in the ___."
   A. overhead bin   B. baggage cart   C. luggage scale   D. passport cover

   → 答案：A（overhead bin）

5. "The ___ keeps my bag secure."
   A. luggage lock   B. luggage strap   C. luggage cover   D. travel adapter

   → 答案：A（luggage lock）
