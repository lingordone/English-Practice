## 语境对话

**场景描述**: 在机场，Sophia 和 Jason 准备登机。他们讨论行李、安检、登机流程。

> **Sophia:** Hey Jason, our plane will land in two hours. Did you pack everything?
> **Jason:** Yes, I packed last night. I also reserved the seats online.
> **Sophia:** Good. We need to check in first, then go through security.
> **Jason:** I hope they don't inspect my bag too carefully.
> **Sophia:** They might scan it. Did you declare anything?
> **Jason:** No, just personal items. Let's load our luggage on the belt.
> **Sophia:** Look, the plane is taxiing on the runway. It will take off soon.
> **Jason:** I love watching planes fly overhead. The pilot will cruise at 30,000 feet.
> **Sophia:** And then descend before we land. I hope we land on time.
> **Jason:** The flight attendant said we might unload some cargo first.
> **Sophia:** Really? I thought they would refuel before we board.
> **Jason:** They do both. The ground crew will patrol the area.
> **Sophia:** Look, they're going to page someone on the loudspeaker.
> **Jason:** They might stamp our passports at the gate.
> **Sophia:** Don't forget to fasten your seatbelt when we board.
> **Jason:** And unfasten it when we land. I can't wait to claim our luggage!
> **Sophia:** After we approach the gate, we'll ascend the jet bridge.
> **Jason:** This is going to be a great trip!

## 语法点

### 动词 + 介词搭配（Verb + Preposition）

- 英语中很多动词需要搭配特定的介词，构成固定短语。
  - **check in**: 办理登机手续
  - **take off**: 起飞
  - **land on**: 降落在
  - **get off**: 下机

- **√** 正确：We need to check in before the flight.
  （我们需要在航班起飞前办理登机手续。）
- **×** 错误：We need to check before the flight.
  （缺少介词 in，表达不完整。）

- **常见搭配**：
  - **arrive at**: 到达（小地点）
  - **arrive in**: 到达（大地点）
  - **get on**: 登机/上车
  - **get off**: 下机/下车
  - **pick up**: 接人/取行李

## 填空练习

1. ""The plane will ___ in ten minutes.""
   A. land   B. fly   C. cruise   D. ascend

   → 答案：A（land）

2. ""I ___ to New York last week.""
   A. flew   B. landed   C. descended   D. approached

   → 答案：A（flew）

3. ""We ___ our luggage at the carousel.""
   A. claimed   B. loaded   C. unloaded   D. inspected

   → 答案：A（claimed）

4. ""The officer ___ my passport.""
   A. stamped   B. packed   C. reserved   D. declared

   → 答案：A（stamped）

5. ""The plane needs to ___ before takeoff.""
   A. refuel   B. unload   C. patrol   D. page

   → 答案：A（refuel）
