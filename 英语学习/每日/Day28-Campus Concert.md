<font color=purple>┃</font> 征服，战胜；克服 v

/ˈkɒŋkə(r)/
![[audio/conquer.mp3]]

### 💬 Example Sentences
1. *"('She managed to **conquer** her stage fright and delivered an amazing performance.', '她成功克服了怯场，献上了一场精彩的表演。')"*
   ![[audio/conquer_ex1.mp3]]
2. *"("The band's goal is to **conquer** the regional music competition.", '这支乐队的目标是赢得地区音乐比赛。')"*
   ![[audio/conquer_ex2.mp3]]

### 🗣️ Dialogue
- Sophia: Ready to **conquer** the stage tonight?
- Jason: Let's do this!

### 📖 Grammar & Usage Note
- **conquer + fear / difficulty**: 克服恐惧/困难。
  √ *She conquered her fear of heights.* — 她克服了对高度的恐惧。
  √ *The army conquered the city in three days.* — 军队三天内攻占了城市。

### ❓ Quiz
- **Fill in the blank**: "She managed to ___ her fear and perform beautifully."
  - Answer: **conquer**

## 🎭 Scenario Dialogue
- **Sophia:** Hey Jason, did you hear our band is going to compete in the campus music festival?
- **Jason:** Yes! But I'm a bit worried. The arrangement is quite complex for our new comrade.
- **Sophia:** Don't worry, we can simplify it. Did you compile the setlist yet?
- **Jason:** Almost. I want to compose a new song, so I added three original tracks.
- **Sophia:** Great concept! The judges will compute scores based on both technique and creativity.
- **Jason:** I confess I'm nervous. It's hard to conceal my anxiety before going on stage.
- **Sophia:** Just remember — don't confine yourself. Connect with the audience and let the music flow.
- **Jason:** Right. And we must conform to the rules — no pre-recorded tracks or the judges will condemn us.
- **Sophia:** Exactly. Also, confirm our performance time with the organizer before Wednesday.
- **Jason:** Already done. As far as safety is concerned, the conduct guidelines are clear.
- **Sophia:** Perfect. I hope our music doesn't confuse anyone with the lighting effects.
- **Jason:** Let's do a sound check. Ready to conquer the stage?
- **Sophia:** Absolutely. Tonight's concert is our moment!
