## 1. print

<font color=purple>|</font> print (v/n)

/prɪnt/
![[audio/print.mp3]]

### Example Sentences
1. *"I need to print my essay before class."* — 我需要在上课前打印我的论文。
   ![[audio/print_ex1.mp3]]
2. *"The library printer is always busy during finals."* — 期末考试期间图书馆的打印机总是很忙。
   ![[audio/print_ex2.mp3]]

### Dialogue
- **Sophia:** Sophia can you help me print my essay?
- **Jason:** Sure but we need to queue for the printer.

### Grammar and Usage Note
- **print out/off** 打印出。√ I printed out my essay. — 我打印了我的论文。× I printed my essay out of the printer. — 语法错误（print out是短语动词，宾语在中间或末尾均可但不可加of the printer）。

### Quiz
- **Fill in the blank**: "I need to ___ my assignment before the deadline."
  - Answer: **print**
