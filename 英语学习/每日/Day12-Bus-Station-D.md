## 语境对话

**场景描述**: 在公交车上，乘客们带着各种物品——背包、公文包、耳机、咖啡……一个急刹车，东西洒了一地。

> **Sophia:** Hey Jason, your **backpack** looks really heavy today. What's in it?
> **Jason:** Just my **briefcase** with some documents and my **duffel** bag for the weekend trip.
> **Sophia:** I see. Did you remember to bring your **charger**? Your phone was dead yesterday.
> **Jason:** Yes, it's in my **handbag**. I also have my **earbuds** for the ride.
> **Sophia:** Good. I brought my **headphones** too, but I prefer using my **earbuds** on the bus.
> **Jason:** Nice. Oh, it's sunny today. I should put on my **sunglasses**.
> **Sophia:** Good idea. I hope it doesn't rain. I forgot my **raincoat** at home.
> **Jason:** At least you have a warm **scarf**. And **gloves** — it's going to be cold tonight.
> **Sophia:** Yes, and my **beanie** too. Wait, is that your **cap** on the floor?
> **Jason:** No, my **cap** is right here. That must be someone else's.
> **Sophia:** Oh no, the bus is braking! Watch out for your **watch**!
> **Jason:** I caught it! Phew. I don't want to lose my **necklace** and **bracelet** — they were gifts.
> **Sophia:** Me neither. I bought a **snack** and a **sandwich** for the trip.
> **Jason:** And I have **coffee** and **juice**. Careful, don't spill the **coffee**!
> **Sophia:** Too late... it's all over my **scarf**! That's the last time I bring **coffee** on the bus.
> **Jason:** Haha, don't worry. You can wash the **scarf** when we get home.

## 语法点

### 名词作定语时的单复数（Singular Noun Adjuncts）

- 英语中用名词修饰另一个名词时，**前面的名词通常用单数形式**，即使概念上是复数。
  - **backpack pocket**（双肩包的口袋）→ backpack 用单数
  - **watch strap**（手表表带）→ watch 用单数
  - **coffee cup**（咖啡杯）→ coffee 用单数

- **√** 正确：She carried a **backpack** on her shoulders.
  （她用双肩包背在肩上。）
- **×** 错误：She carried a **backpacks** on her shoulders.
  （误用复数形式，不符合英语表达习惯。）

- **注意**：复数名词作定语时通常保留复数形式的情况：
  - **glasses case**（眼镜盒）→ glasses 本身是复数
  - **clothes shop**（服装店）→ clothes 本身是复数

## 填空练习

1. "She carried a heavy ___ on her back to the bus station."
   A. backpack   B. briefcase   C. handbag   D. duffel

   → 答案：A（backpack = 双肩背包）

2. "He forgot his ___ and his phone died on the bus."
   A. charger   B. earbuds   C. headphones   D. watch

   → 答案：A（charger = 充电器）

3. "She wore a warm ___ around her neck in winter."
   A. beanie   B. cap   C. scarf   D. gloves

   → 答案：C（scarf = 围巾）

4. "He spilled his ___ all over his shirt when the bus braked."
   A. juice   B. coffee   C. snack   D. sandwich

   → 答案：B（coffee = 咖啡）

5. "She wore a silver ___ with a pendant around her neck."
   A. bracelet   B. watch   C. necklace   D. cap

   → 答案：C（necklace = 项链）
