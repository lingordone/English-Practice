## 📐 语法点

### 可数名词与不可数名词 (Countable & Uncountable Nouns)

本单元学习了大量与旅行相关的物品名词，包括可数和不可数名词。

**可数名词 (Countable Nouns):**
- ticket, timetable, suitcase, backpack, briefcase, handbag, wallet, purse, coin, note, passport, seat, berth, cushion, blanket, pillow, meal
- 有复数形式，前面可以加 a/an 或数字
- *"I bought **two tickets** for the journey."* — 我买了两张旅途车票。

**不可数名词 (Uncountable Nouns):**
- luggage, money
- 没有复数形式，前面不能加 a/an
- *"The **luggage** is too heavy."* — 行李太重了。（不说 luggages）
- *"I need some **money**."* — 我需要一些钱。（不说 moneys）

**常见错误：**
- × "I have many luggages." → ✅ "I have a lot of luggage."
- × "She gave me two monies." → ✅ "She gave me two notes/coins."
- × "He lost his identities card." → ✅ "He lost his identity card."
