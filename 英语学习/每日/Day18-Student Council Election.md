<font color=purple>┃</font> 粗糙的；粗略的；艰难的 adj

/rʌf/
![[audio/rough.mp3]]

### 💬 Example Sentences
1. *"The **rough** road made the bus ride uncomfortable."* — 崎岖不平的道路让公交旅程很不舒服。。
   ![[audio/rough_ex1.mp3]]
2. *"Let's make a **rough** draft of the speech first."* — 我们先写个演讲稿的粗略草稿。。
   ![[audio/rough_ex2.mp3]]

### 🗣️ Dialogue
- **Sophia:** The path up the ridge is quite rough. Are you sure about the booth location?
- **Jason:** It's a rough walk, but the visibility is worth it.

### 📖 Grammar & Usage Note
- **rough + draft/estimate/surface**: 粗略的…/粗糙的…。修饰名词表示不精细或不平滑。
  - √ *"We only have a rough idea of the budget."* — 我们只有预算的大概想法。。
  - × *"The rough of the plan needs more detail."* — rough是形容词，不能作名词用。。

### ❓ Quiz
- **Fill in the blank**: "The _____ surface of the wall needs sanding."
  - Answer: **rough**

## 🎭 Scenario Dialogue
- **Sophia:** Hey Jason, the student council election is next week. We need to rally at least 200 students for our campaign.
- **Jason:** I know. The ratio of supporters to opponents is not great. Our rival Mike is already rapid with his posters.
- **Sophia:** His campaign designs look rigid and boring. We need something fresh and exciting.
- **Jason:** I will refer our plan to the advisor and reply to the forum questions tonight.
- **Sophia:** Great. I will react to every comment on social media. But first I need to relax and renew my energy.
- **Jason:** No time for that! We must get ready. The realm of student politics waits for no one.
- **Sophia:** Okay, I will not rebel against your timeline. Let me reach out to more people.
- **Jason:** Good idea. We can set up a booth on the ridge near the library. It is a rough area but has high foot traffic.
- **Sophia:** That is a rough plan but I trust you. By the way, could we use a robot to hand out flyers? That would be fun.
- **Jason:** Now you are talking! And we can even roast some snacks for volunteers, just no rifles at the rally!
- **Sophia:** Haha, your humor has a wide range. Let us go win this election!
